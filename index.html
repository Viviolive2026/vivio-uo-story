Getting started · University of Oregon setup

Rapid onboarding. Two steps. Pilot timeline scoped for UO.

Begin in the Discord / GroupMe community students already use — no app to download, no opt-in cliff. Opt-in only, with no education records on the consumer surface. SIS integration and dashboards are Phase 2, after data-security review.

1

Share your student roster

Standard CSV export — works with StarRez, Banner, Workday, Colleague, whatever UO Housing uses. Upload once to get started; submit updates anytime, refreshed weekly during the pilot.

One-time setup · ~5 minutes
2

Meet students where they already are

Instrument the hall's existing Discord/GroupMe server, or Vivio stands one up — zero install, the lowest-friction path. For students who prefer a dedicated app, a QR card in move-in packets opens the same experience, same signal, same scoring. Combined coverage target: 70%+.

Primary: Discord/GroupMe · App optional

Phase 2 — on roadmap: SIS integration (Banner, Workday, StarRez), embedded staff dashboards, self-serve res-life admin. Available post-pilot, after data-security review.
